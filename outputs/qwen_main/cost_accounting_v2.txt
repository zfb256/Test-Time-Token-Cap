Notes:
- B_m=384, B_l=768; savings measured against fixed long
  WITHOUT a medium pass (the honest deployment baseline).
- rerun: routed examples pay B_m + B_l (the medium trace is required
  as routing evidence and is then discarded).
- resume: routed examples pay B_m + (B_l - B_m); requires the system
  to continue the truncated trace (08_run_continuation.py validates
  and quantifies agreement with independent long reruns).
  Naturally terminated traces are a no-op and incur no continuation
  tokens even if selected by a routing rule.
- estimated_actual_tokens_resume uses the independent long trace
  length as a continuation-length proxy; it is not a measured
  serving cost from inference_long_resume.jsonl.
- The paper must state which accounting it uses; the original 35%
  figure is only valid under resume accounting.
