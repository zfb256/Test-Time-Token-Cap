Key quantities:
- repair_rate_*: among base complete-but-wrong problems, how often the
  sampled extended run produces a correct majority (or any) answer.
  Under the old greedy protocol this was 0 by construction. Under
  independent sampling it is an observable transition, but not by
  itself a token-budget effect: a same-budget resampling control is
  required. Tie-lower/upper repair counts are reported explicitly.
- helpful_from_truncated vs helpful_from_complete_wrong: taxonomy of
  helpful cases via independent observable predicates. Mixed sampled
  sets and complete-but-unparseable/aggregate-error sets are reported
  separately, so the four helpful_from_* columns partition all helpful
  cases instead of hiding a residual category.
